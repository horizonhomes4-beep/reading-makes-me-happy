Student Gmail Page

Connect your Gmail, inspect one message, and choose whether to share it

1. Sign in and connect Gmail

Use your own Google account. Google's official permission screen will ask for Gmail read-only access. This page never asks for your Google password directly.

Not signed in.

2. Read your latest email

After you connect Gmail, select the button to retrieve the newest email. The content is shown here for you to inspect before sharing.

3. Share this email with the instructor

Nothing is sent to the instructor until you press the share button below. The instructor will be able to see the sender, subject and message body you share.

You can review the content above and decide not to share it. Only one selected email is shared at a time.

4. Import and select SMS records

Import a student-prepared CSV or JSON export from your phone. Nothing is uploaded until you select records and press Share.

No SMS records imported.

5. Import and select call-history records

Import a student-prepared CSV or JSON call-history export. Review and select individual calls before sharing.

No call records imported.

6. Activity log

Page loaded. No Gmail content has been accessed.

7. Privacy and limitations

This teaching demo requests Gmail readonly permission. It retrieves one latest message only after a student action. Sharing is a separate explicit action. It does not read WhatsApp, TikTok, Facebook or other apps; it does not bypass encryption; and it does not run a hidden background collector. Attachments are not included. Message body display is limited to 20,000 characters. SMS and call history are only imported from files the student chooses; this web app cannot read the phone inbox or call log directly. No records are shared until the student selects them and confirms.
